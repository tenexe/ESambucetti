Exequiel Sambucetti

Director / Editor / Producer





My experience brings together film directing, agency work as a Senior Editor, and freelance production for brands and companies. I move comfortably between the creative and the operational, and I define myself by versatility, narrative judgment, and attention to process.
For complex projects, I build custom teams and integrate disciplines such as motion graphics, 3D, and VFX as needed.

I trained at the Universidad del Cine in Buenos Aires and complemented that foundation with studies in screenwriting, production, and emerging technologies. In 2015 I directed Homunculus , a short film that won a national award for emerging filmmakers. It was released theatrically in Argentina and later screened at festivals locally and abroad.
