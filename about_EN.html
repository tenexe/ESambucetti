I was born in 1988 in Buenos Aires, Argentina. I trained as a Film Director at Universidad del Cine, where I graduated in 2011. Shortly after, I won the INCAA “Historias Breves” Competition —a national award aimed at emerging talent— which allowed me to direct my short film Homúnculo, released in theaters in 2015 and screened at festivals across Latin America and Europe.

I worked as a Senior Editor at an agency dedicated to a world-leading brand, in high-demand environments with multiple simultaneous campaigns and international standards. In parallel, and as a freelancer, I work as a Producer and Director for both agencies and direct clients, developing advertising and institutional pieces end-to-end. Throughout my career I have also taken on roles such as assistant director, VFX supervisor, and sound recordist. Before fully dedicating myself to audiovisual work, I spent years in a textile printing company, managing client relationships and coordinating with design and production teams.

Complementing this path, I expanded my training through courses in Screenwriting (Juan José Campanella, Aída Bortnik, Fernando Castets), TV Production (Pablo Culell), Web Development (Coderhouse), Audiovisual Production & Business (Alejandro Cacetta), and Virtual Production with Unreal Engine.

All of this defines my current profile: creative and operational versatility, goal-oriented, combining narrative, technique, and management to turn ideas into concrete, well-executed projects.
